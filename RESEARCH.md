# Course research and decisions

Reviewed on 6 October 2026. Puro is a personal study tool for Rajin and Labbaiqua, with university and workplace communication as the goal. The research guided sequencing and practice design. It does not establish equivalence to a paid course or guarantee fluency.

## Course benchmarks

[Suomen mestari 1, official sample](https://finnlectura.fi/media/sites/2/suomen-mestari-1-uudistettu-naytesivut-3.pdf) shows a beginner sequence grounded in introductions, daily life, family, housing, travel, food, and work. Puro follows a similar progression in topic difficulty, with original material and separate lessons for time, noun stems, plural forms, and useful requests. No paid textbook exercises were reproduced.

[FinnishPod101's Level 1 course](https://www.finnishpod101.com/lesson-library/level-1-finnish) combines dialogue and audio material with assessment. Its [learning service](https://www.finnishpod101.com/about-us/) also offers teacher support in some plans. Puro pairs explanation with listening, recall, and production, but does not claim the teacher feedback or recorded audio of that service.

[Aalto University's introductory course](https://openlearning.aalto.fi/mod/page/view.php?id=3365&lang=fi) provides a useful university learning reference and additional beginner practice. Some interactive activities require enrollment. Puro's missions direct learners to external material and give a specific listening or speaking purpose instead of simply presenting a directory of links.

## Practice method

The [Association for Psychological Science review of study strategies](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/observer/which-study-strategies-make-the-grade-2) supports practice testing and distributed practice as useful learning approaches. Puro asks learners to recall before revealing, revisits errors after a delay, and varies questions in cumulative checks. Its intervals are a simple scheduling heuristic, not a calibrated prediction of forgetting.

[Kielibuusti's guidance on Finnish at work](https://www.kielibuusti.fi/en/learn-finnish/language-learning-tips/learn-finnish-at-work) emphasizes opportunities to learn in everyday working situations. Puro includes clarification requests, progress updates, campus inquiries, scheduling, email, and partner tasks with information to exchange. These tasks are original applications of that practical focus.

## Grammar and usage references

Each lesson links to a relevant reference where available. Beginner forms were cross-checked against Uusi kielemme's explanations of [genitive](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-grammar/finnish-cases/grammatical-cases/the-genitive-case-genetiivi), [nominative plural](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-grammar/finnish-cases/grammatical-cases/the-t-plural-t-monikko-plural-nominative), [noun inflection](https://uusikielemme.fi/inflection-of-nouns-finnish-grammar), [liking constructions](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-grammar/syntax/rections/tykata-and-pitaa-saying-you-like-things-in-finnish), [imperatives](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-grammar/verbs/verb-tenses-and-moods/the-imperative-mood-imperatiivi-tule-mene-syo), [food and partitive objects](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-grammar/syntax/object-sentences/mass-nouns-as-objects-food-and-the-partitive), [numbers](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-vocabulary/vocabulary-lists/the-numbers-in-finnish-numerot), and [time expressions](https://uusikielemme.fi/finnish-vocabulary/vocabulary-lists/telling-the-time-in-finnish-mita-kello-on).

Notes identify standard and spoken forms. Typed exercises ask for a taught form or example, because an unrestricted English translation can have several valid Finnish answers. Case explanations distinguish noun phrases, predicate adjectives, objects, and rections rather than presenting one universal ending rule.

## Authentic input and advanced study

[Yle's beginner material](https://yle.fi/aihe/oppiminen/suomen-kielen-alkeet), [Selkouutiset](https://yle.fi/selkouutiset), and [Areena](https://areena.yle.fi/) provide listening beyond synthesized examples. The missions move from identifying familiar phrases to retelling, argument analysis, and synthesis of two sources. Program availability, subtitles, and regional access vary.

[Kielibuusti's material finder](https://www.kielibuusti.fi/en/learn-finnish/learning-materials-and-courses/find-online-learning-materials) helps extend independent study. [InfoFinland's level descriptions](https://infofinland.fi/en/finnish-and-swedish/language-course-levels) inform the broad progression and reflection prompts. A fixed set of lessons cannot certify those levels. For advanced study, the app asks learners to work with unfamiliar material and seek feedback on longer writing and spontaneous speech.

No external account is connected to Puro. External resources open in their own tabs; the app does not embed paid material, purchase subscriptions, or transmit learner progress.
